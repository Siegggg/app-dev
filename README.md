#  Attack on Titan / Shingeki no Kyojin

## Overview
  Attack on Titan is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by *Hajime Isayama*.  
It first aired in **2013** and quickly became one of the most popular anime worldwide.

---

## Plot Summary
Humanity lives inside enormous walled cities to protect themselves from man-eating humanoid creatures known as **Titans**.  
The story follows **Eren Yeager**, **Mikasa Ackerman**, and **Armin Arlert**, who join the **Survey Corps** to fight the Titans and uncover the mysteries of their world.

---

## Main Characters
- **Eren Yeager** – A determined young man seeking freedom beyond the walls.  
- **Mikasa Ackerman** – Eren’s adoptive sister and one of humanity’s most skilled soldiers.  
- **Armin Arlert** – A strategist with a kind heart and a sharp mind.  
- **Levi Ackerman** – Humanity’s strongest soldier and captain of the Special Operations Squad.
